Customer Intelligence

Segmentation and retention, keyed on customer_unique_id — the person — not customer_id, which Olist re-issues for every order.

3.0% repeat rate is real, not a broken metric. Olist is a one-and-done marketplace: 90,557 of 93,358 customers order exactly once. Repeat buyers are worth 1.9× more (R$ 308.53 vs R$ 160.73 lifetime value), so retention is the opportunity — but customer churn cannot be modelled here, which is why the ML layer targets seller churn instead.

Operations & Delivery

Delivery performance and what it costs. This page carries the finding the whole platform is built on.

What a late delivery costs

Negative-review rate and average score by delivery outcome. Two charts on one shared axis rather than one dual-axis chart, so neither scale distorts the other.

Delivery risk is revenue risk. Negative reviews climb from 8.9% when an order arrives 10+ days early to 80.0% when it is 8–15 days late — monotonically, across 95,824 reviewed orders. A late delivery costs roughly 2 full stars. This is why the platform predicts delays before they happen rather than reporting them afterwards.
